The smallest line-up is a duo with a pianist. I do not sing over backing tracks: live accompaniment reacts to the room, drops the volume for a speech and stretches a song when the schedule shifts.

In the duo I am accompanied by pianist Jaroslav Bárta or other pianists I play with regularly. Add double bass and drums and you have the trio and quartet; add a horn section and you have the Jazz Combo.

For most corporate evenings and receptions, a duo or trio is enough. I recommend a band with drums where people will dance, and the Jazz Combo for a large hall and a formal programme.

What I sing

The repertoire is built on jazz and swing standards, ballads and Czech classics. Most of it is sung in English. Our website shows 83 songs; altogether we play more than a hundred.

  • Swing and standards: Fly Me to the Moon, The Way You Look Tonight, Theme from New York, New York, I've Got You Under My Skin
  • Ballads: At Last, What A Wonderful World, Moon River, My Way
  • Modern songs in jazz arrangements: Valerie, Isn't She Lovely, Thinking Out Loud
  • Czech classics: Jen pro ten dnešní den, Pramínek vlasů, Lady Carneval

We learn a song on request to order, ideally with three weeks' notice. Which style suits which part of the evening is covered in jazz styles: swing, bossa nova, lounge.

Events I sing at

  • Corporate evenings and galas. Quieter for the welcome drink and dinner, livelier swing later. We time the programme around speeches and awards.
  • Receptions and gallery openings. The duo fits into a gallery and plays so that the music never drowns out conversation.
  • Hotels and restaurants. Dinner evenings and regular residencies; more in live jazz for restaurants and hotels.
  • Weddings. For the ceremony, dinner and the first dance. We learn your song on request.
  • Private parties. Birthdays and anniversaries in a restaurant, a private room or a garden.

Where you can hear me live

Before you book a singer, it is best to hear them. At Jazz Club Reduta in Prague I regularly sing a Tony Bennett Tribute programme with the quartet and an evening of duets called Jazz Duets. Current dates are in our concert listings.

How a performance works

  • Enquiry. Send the date, venue and your idea of the programme. We send a quote within 24 hours.
  • Programme. We build the repertoire around the character of the event and the timing of speeches or awards.
  • Arrival. We arrive 60 minutes before the start; set-up takes about 30 minutes.
  • Sound. We bring our own professional BOSE PA for events of up to 80 to 100 guests. In a larger hall we work with a sound engineer.
  • Volume. During dinner I sing so that guests can talk without raising their voices.
  • Break. A standard evening is 2 hours of live music with one 15-minute break; we place the break to suit your programme.

No backing tracks

Singer with a track, or with a pianist?

A singer with a backing track cannot react to the room. A pianist slows down, goes quiet for a speech and stretches a song while everyone waits for the cake. That is why I never sing to playback, and the smallest line-up is the duo.

Line-up and price

The duo, vocals and piano, costs from CZK 15,000 for a standard evening. The trio with double bass costs from CZK 20,000, the quartet from CZK 27,000 and the Jazz Combo from CZK 50,000.

A standard evening means 2 hours of live music with one 15-minute break, including the BOSE PA and transport within Prague; for the Jazz Combo, the PA with a sound engineer is priced according to the venue. Outside Prague we charge CZK 7/km for the duo and trio. Full details are in our price list.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire a jazz singer in Prague?

The smallest line-up, vocals with piano, costs from CZK 15,000 for a standard evening of 2 hours of live music with one break, including the PA and transport within Prague. With double bass it is from CZK 20,000.

Do you sing without a band, to a backing track?

No. I always sing live with accompaniment, and the smallest line-up is the duo with a pianist. We do not use backing tracks or playback.

Which songs do you sing?

Jazz and swing standards, ballads, modern songs in jazz arrangements and Czech classics, mostly in English. We learn a requested song to order, ideally with three weeks' notice.

Where can we hear you live?

Regularly at Jazz Club Reduta in Prague. Current concert dates are listed on our website.

Do you also sing at weddings?

Yes. A duo or trio suits the ceremony best; for an evening party with dancing, the quartet with drums.
