Hiring live music in Prague: what to expect

Five steps lead from enquiry to the first note. Knowing what to expect at each one saves you two weeks of emails and unpleasant surprises on the day.

DK
David Kleňha
Jazz singer & bandleader · 8 min read · 8 August 2026

Booking a band is not complicated, but there are a few points where it gets held up unnecessarily. Usually because the enquiry lacks the details needed to produce a quote, and a week of back-and-forth follows. This article sets it out so that one email is enough.

Step 1: The enquiry — what to include

Six details are enough. With them we can send a specific price; without them, only a range.

Step 2: The quote — within 24 hours

We reply within 24 hours and the quote contains:

  • Confirmation of whether the date is free.
  • The recommended line-up and why.
  • A specific price, not a range — including transport.
  • Number and length of sets.
  • Technical requirements for that line-up.

You already know the ranges from the price list: duo CZK 10,000–14,000, trio CZK 16,000–20,000, quartet CZK 22,000–26,000, quintet CZK 28,000–32,000 for a standard evening in Prague.

Tip

Book early, but do ask late as well

December dates fill from September and summer dates from spring. At the same time, slots do open up even a week ahead — if a date is close, just ask. The answer comes just as fast.

Step 3: Confirmation and contract

The date is reserved as soon as you confirm in writing. Corporate events are usually followed by a contract or purchase order covering:

  • Date, start and end time of the performance.
  • The exact line-up — by name, not just headcount.
  • Number and length of sets.
  • Total price including transport, and invoicing terms.
  • Who provides the PA system.
  • Cancellation terms.

For companies working with framework agreements or through an agency, this can also be handled as a purchase order referencing the framework — we have experience with that.

Step 4: Before the event

About a week ahead we confirm the details that tend to change by then:

  • Running order — when to play, when to break, when the speeches happen.
  • On-site contact — who lets the band in and who gives signals.
  • Requested songs — if something needs learning, ideally tell us three weeks ahead.
  • Loading access and parking.
  • Noise limits and the hour by which music must stop.

Common misunderstandings

  • "The band plays three hours" ≠ "plays without stopping". Three hours means three 45-minute sets with breaks. Continuous playing is not physically possible and would not serve the event.
  • An evening rate is not an hourly rate. A shorter programme is not automatically cheaper — the musicians cannot take another booking that day.
  • The venue's "in-house PA" may not be usable. A conference system for a speaker is not enough for a band. Check that well before the day.
  • The dance floor does not fill itself. If you want dancing you need a quartet and time in the programme — see background jazz vs dance music.

International clients

A large share of corporate events in Prague hotels is for international guests. We communicate in Czech, English and German, and the repertoire is international — jazz standards work equally well with English- and German-speaking audiences.

Frequently asked questions

What should I include in an enquiry?

The date, start and end time of the performance, the venue address, the number of guests, the type of event and whether the venue has its own PA. With those we send a specific price within 24 hours.

How far in advance should a band be booked?

December dates fill from September and summer dates from spring. Slots do open up even a week ahead, so it is worth asking late as well.

What should a contract with a band cover?

The date and time of the performance, the exact line-up, the number and length of sets, the total price including transport, who provides the PA and the cancellation terms.

Does a three-hour programme mean playing without a break?

No. A standard three-hour programme is three 45-minute sets with breaks of 15 to 20 minutes. Continuous playing is not possible and would not benefit the event.

Do you work in English or German?

Yes, we communicate in Czech, English and German. The websites for all line-ups are available in all three languages.
