How much does a jazz band cost for a corporate event

A jazz trio in Prague costs CZK 16,000 to 20,000 for a standard evening — three 45-minute sets, our own PA system and transport within Prague included. Here is the full price list.

Most bands do not publish prices and refer you to an individual enquiry. That is understandable — every event is different. It also means that an event manager putting together a budget has nothing to work with. So here are our jazz band prices, openly.

I am David Kleňha, a jazz singer and bandleader based in Prague. Since 2015 we have played more than 320 events — corporate evenings, receptions, gallery openings, hotel nights and weddings. We perform in line-ups from duo to quintet and all prices below are valid for 2026.

Transport outside Prague: why larger line-ups cost double

Transport is not charged per band but per vehicle. A duo and a trio fit into one car along with the PA system. A quartet also carries a drum kit, and a quintet is five musicians with instruments — neither fits into one vehicle, so we travel in two.

How the CZK 6/km rate is calculated

Transport is not a source of profit — it is a pass-through of actual costs: fuel, the motorway vignette and any parking in city centres. So here are the figures the rate is built from.

ItemValue
Consumption of a fully loaded vehicle9.5 l / 100 km
Petrol priceCZK 45 / l
Fuel per 100 kmCZK 428
Fuel per 1 kmCZK 4.28
Motorway vignette and parkingthe remaining CZK 1.72/km
Rate chargedCZK 6/km per vehicle

A fully loaded vehicle — equipment, instruments and musicians — uses more fuel than an empty car, which is why the calculation uses 9.5 litres per 100 kilometres. The rate does not include time spent travelling or wear on the vehicle; those are part of the fee, not of transport.

Kilometres are counted from Prague to the venue and back. For an event in Brno (roughly 410 km there and back) that means CZK 2,460 for a trio and CZK 4,920 for a quartet. It is not a surcharge for band size — it is the real cost of two cars.

One exception is worth mentioning: if the organiser provides the PA for a quartet, we are not carrying equipment and can sometimes fit into one vehicle. Mention it in your enquiry and we will reflect it in the quote.

Why prices differ by line-up

The price is essentially the musicians' fees plus equipment and transport. Each additional musician is one more professional fee — which is why the gap between a duo and a quintet is roughly double. But it is not only about headcount: the sound changes, and so does what the music does to the room.

Duo — CZK 10,000 to 14,000

Vocals and piano. The quietest and most space-efficient option, ideal for dinner, a reception or a gallery opening. It needs a floor area of 3 × 2.5 m. More at jazzduo.cz.

Trio — CZK 16,000 to 20,000

Vocals, piano and double bass. The most frequently booked line-up for corporate evenings. The double bass gives the music a bottom end and a full sound without adding volume. The line-up is David Kleňha (vocals), Jaroslav Bárta (piano) and Jan Greifoner (double bass).

Quartet — CZK 22,000 to 26,000

Trio plus drums (Branko Križek). Drums are the dividing line between background music and dance music. If you expect dancing, a quartet is the minimum — see jazzquartet.cz.

Quintet — CZK 28,000 to 32,000

Quartet plus a horn section, usually saxophone or clarinet, trumpet on request. The richest sound for large corporate parties and gala evenings. Details at jazzquintet.cz.

How to compare quotes

Band prices are not comparable until you know what is inside them. Before comparing, find out for each band:

  • How many minutes of music the price covers — three 45-minute sets is a different volume from two hours.
  • Whether a PA system is included and how many guests it covers.
  • How transport is calculated — flat fee or per kilometre.
  • Whether the band plays live or uses backing tracks.
  • How many musicians will actually arrive, and in what line-up.

The difference between CZK 16,000 and CZK 25,000 is often not quality but the fact that one price includes equipment and the other does not.

When to enquire

December (corporate Christmas parties) and May to September are the busiest. For a December date, ask in September; for a summer event, in spring. Last-minute dates do come up, but the choice of line-up is narrower.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a jazz band cost for a corporate event in Prague?

A jazz duo costs CZK 10,000 to 14,000, a trio CZK 16,000 to 20,000, a quartet CZK 22,000 to 26,000 and a quintet CZK 28,000 to 32,000. Prices are for a standard evening in Prague, meaning three 45-minute sets including our own PA system and transport within Prague.

What is included in the price?

Three 45-minute sets, our own active PA system for events of up to 80 to 100 guests, transport within Prague, programme preparation tailored to the event, and live playing with no backing tracks.

How much is transport outside Prague?

Transport is charged by the number of vehicles: a duo and a trio travel in one vehicle at CZK 6 per kilometre, a quartet and a quintet in two vehicles at CZK 12 per kilometre. The CZK 6/km rate passes through actual costs — a fully loaded vehicle uses 9.5 litres per 100 km, and at a petrol price of CZK 45 per litre that is CZK 4.28 per kilometre in fuel, with the remainder covering the motorway vignette and parking. Kilometres are counted from Prague and back; the performance fee itself stays the same.

How quickly do you send a quote?

We send a specific quote for your date within 24 hours of your enquiry. We need the date, venue, guest count and approximate programme length.

Can we book a shorter programme than three hours?

Yes. Gallery openings and receptions are often two 45-minute sets. The price of a shorter programme is agreed individually depending on the date and venue.
