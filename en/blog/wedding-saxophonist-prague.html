Saxophone at your wedding: when it works and in which line-up

A wedding saxophonist brings a melodic, festive sound to the ceremony and dinner. With us, though, the saxophone never plays alone: it is always part of a band with a singer, in the quartet or in the Jazz Combo.

DK
David Kleňha
Jazz singer & bandleader · 7 min read · 7 November 2026

People looking for a saxophonist for their wedding often picture one musician playing over a backing track. We do not offer that. We are a vocal-led jazz band, and the saxophone is one more voice in it: it plays melodies and solos, and answers the singer.

In the quartet, the saxophone plays instead of drums. In the Jazz Combo, two saxophones play together with trumpet, trombone and drums and give the band a big band sound.

We are happy to advise based on your wedding programme. If you want both, saxophone for the ceremony and drums for dancing, the answer is a smaller Jazz Combo line-up: vocals, piano, double bass, drums and saxophone, with more horns if you like.

When a wedding saxophonist makes sense

  • Guests arriving. Calm instrumentals with the saxophone carrying the melody while guests take their seats.
  • Ceremony. The saxophone can carry the melody for the bride's entrance and the instrumental music during the signing. The quartet needs 4 × 3 m, so check that it fits the ceremony space.
  • Congratulations and drinks. Bossa nova with saxophone is a natural backdrop for a glass and a conversation.
  • Dinner. Ballads and mid-tempo standards, with the saxophone taking the melody and trading phrases with the voice.
  • Dancing. For the dance floor, the quartet with drums or the Jazz Combo, which has both saxophones and drums, works better.

Songs where the saxophone shines

The saxophone sits well on swing standards, bossa nova and soulful ballads. From our repertoire, for example:

  • Ceremony and dinner: At Last, Moon River, Georgia on My Mind, What A Wonderful World
  • Drinks reception: The Girl From Ipanema, How Insensitive, Sway
  • Evening: Feeling Good, Just The Two of Us, Valerie, Route 66

Songs for the whole day are listed in our wedding songs list, and the ceremony is covered in detail in wedding ceremony music.

How to plan the songs with saxophone

Send us the songs you want to hear and mark where the saxophone should take centre stage, for example for the bride's entrance or the first dance. We then build the programme so that the voice and the saxophone alternate and nothing repeats. If the saxophone should also play during the signing, we fit the length of the piece to the ceremony.

A song that is not in our repertoire we learn to order, ideally with three weeks' notice.

Why we work this way

The saxophone never plays alone

We are a vocal-led band and always play live, with no backing tracks. Our smallest line-up is the duo, vocals and piano. We add the saxophone to a band with double bass, where it has rhythm and harmony to lean on.

Saxophone for corporate events too

The quartet with saxophone is not only a wedding line-up. It suits a hotel dinner, a reception or a swing evening for a company, where people listen more than they dance.

For gala dinners and company anniversaries with dancing, we recommend the Jazz Combo with its full horn section. How to plan the music at a company event is covered in corporate party band in Prague.

Line-up and price

The quartet with saxophone costs from CZK 27,000 for a standard evening of 2 hours of live music with one 15-minute break, including our own BOSE PA and transport within Prague. The Jazz Combo starts from CZK 50,000 with the full eight-piece line-up; smaller line-ups with saxophone and drums are quoted individually.

Ceremony music is arranged as a separate block according to the venue and the timing of the day. Transport outside Prague is charged per vehicle and shown in the quote, which we send within 24 hours. All prices are in our jazz band prices.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding band with saxophone cost in Prague?

Our saxophone plays in a quartet with vocals, piano and double bass, which costs from CZK 27,000 for a standard evening, including the PA and transport within Prague. We do not offer a solo saxophonist.

Do you offer a solo saxophone for the ceremony?

No. The saxophone always plays with the band and a singer. If you want a smaller line-up for the ceremony, the smallest is the duo with vocals and piano.

Can the quartet have both saxophone and drums?

The quartet has either saxophone or drums. If you want both, that is a smaller Jazz Combo line-up, which we quote individually.

Do you play with backing tracks?

No. Everything is played live, with no backing tracks.

Is a saxophone a good choice for a corporate event?

Yes. The quartet with saxophone is an elegant choice for a dinner, a reception or a swing evening. For a company event with dancing, we recommend the quartet with drums or the Jazz Combo.

Will a male or a female saxophonist play?

The line-up varies with the date. If you have a preference, mention it in your enquiry and we will tell you who plays on your date.
